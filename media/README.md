# Photos and recordings

We use these photos and recordings to revisit the process at Yards and explain the equipment we are working around. Our discovery photos show the packing lanes and process notes, while the October 7 photos give us closer views of the cans and conveyor mechanisms.

The September 4 audio goes with our [transcripts and meeting notes](../docs/meetings/yards/README.md). The videos from our October visit are linked in the [video index](videos/README.md). Most of the photos are HEIC files, so you may need a compatible viewer to open them.
