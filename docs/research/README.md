# Discovery and domain research

We started by studying Yards' canning and variety-pack processes so we could understand where errors occur and which parts of the workflow could benefit from automation. These notes and portfolios bring together our research, observations, and early solution ideas.

Our approach has changed as we learned more from Yards. The portfolios explain that development, while our [current requirements](../requirements/README.md) describe what we are focusing on now.
